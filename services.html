Interior Walls

Wall Paint • Flat / Matte, Velvet / Eggshell, or Satin

Interior walls need the finish matched to traffic and cleanability. Matte hides imperfections, eggshell or velvet balances appearance and cleaning, and satin is better for kitchens, bathrooms, and high-use areas.

Finish Levels & Shine

Flat / Matte

Low shine and best at hiding imperfections.

  • Best for: ceilings, bedrooms, living rooms
  • Look: soft and modern
  • Cleanability: lower

Velvet / Eggshell

Balanced appearance with better cleanability than matte.

  • Best for: walls in social areas
  • Look: soft sheen
  • Cleanability: medium

Low-Lustre / Satin

More durable for moisture and higher-traffic rooms.

  • Best for: kitchens, bathrooms, hallways
  • Look: medium sheen
  • Cleanability: high

Semi-Gloss

Strong and highly washable for trim, doors, and wet areas.

  • Best for: trim, doors, wet areas
  • Look: bright sheen
  • Cleanability: very high

Gloss / High-Gloss

Maximum shine that highlights details and prep quality.

  • Best for: accents, special trim, select cabinets
  • Look: high shine
  • Cleanability: maximum
