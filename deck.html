Deck Painting & Stain

Lupa Painting protects and beautifies decks, porches and outdoor stairs with professional painting and staining services. We help your outdoor spaces stand up to New England sun, rain and snow while looking clean, rich and inviting.

Deck painting & staining options

From transparent stains to solid deck coatings, we help you choose the right protection and look for your space.

Deck cleaning & prep

Power washing and surface cleaning to remove dirt, mildew and loose fibers before any coating is applied.

Transparent & semi-transparent stain

Enhances the natural wood grain while adding UV and moisture protection to your deck boards and railings.

Solid deck stain & coatings

Covers older wood with rich color and uniform finish, helping to hide imperfections and previous repairs.

Deck painting

Durable deck paints and porch enamels for high-traffic areas like steps, landings and covered porches.

Railings, spindles & columns

Detailed prep and coating of railings, balusters and posts for a clean, finished look around your deck.

Repairs & maintenance

Minor board replacement, screw and nail setting, and caulking to extend the life of your deck before coating.

Our Deck Painting & Staining Process

Outdoor wood takes a beating from weather, so we start with thorough inspection and surface preparation. We look for loose boards, popped nails, mildew, gray weathered wood and peeling coatings.

We then handle cleaning and washing, usually with power washing and appropriate cleaners to remove dirt, algae and old, failing stain. After the deck dries, we sand rough spots and feather peeling areas for a smoother finish.

Based on how you use the space and the current condition of the wood, we help you choose between transparent, semi-transparent or solid stains, or a durable porch and floor paint. We use professional-grade products made for decks and horizontal surfaces exposed to the elements.

Finally, we apply the coating following manufacturer specs for coverage and drying time, paying attention to railings, steps and edges. You get a refreshed deck that looks great and is easier to clean and maintain season after season.
