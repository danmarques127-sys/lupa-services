Choosing the Right Carpentry Solution

Some projects only need a clean repair, while others are better solved with full replacement or a new installation. We help you choose the best option based on condition, budget, and the final look you want.

Carpentry Care & Maintenance Tips

  • Check caulked joints and seams seasonally
  • Keep wood surfaces dry and clean, especially near sinks and windows
  • Repaint or reseal exterior wood when finishes begin to wear
  • Let us know if you notice movement, gaps, or loose trim
Pro Tip: Small gaps, loose trim, and water exposure are easier and cheaper to fix early before they turn into larger repairs.

Repairs

Best for damaged trim, loose boards, minor framing issues, baseboards, and carpentry fixes that can be restored cleanly.

Replacement

Ideal when parts are cracked, swollen, rotted, or too worn to repair, often the best option for long-term durability.

New Installations

For upgrades like doors, cabinets, trim packages, deck boards, and finish carpentry details that improve function and appearance.
