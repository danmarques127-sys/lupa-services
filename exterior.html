Exterior Painting Services

Lupa Painting provides high-quality exterior painting services designed to protect and enhance your home. From siding and trim to decks, fences and porches, we use durable, weather-resistant materials to make your house look clean, fresh and long-lasting.

What we can paint on your home's exterior

Siding, trim, doors, decks, fences and more — using long-lasting weather-resistant coatings.

Siding repaint

Full siding painting for wood, vinyl, composite or aluminum surfaces, improving curb appeal.

Exterior trim & fascia

Sanding, caulking and repainting of exterior trim, fascia boards and decorative woodwork.

Entry & garage doors

Refreshing front doors, shutters and garage doors with durable finishes that resist sun and weather.

Deck & porch refinishing

Staining or painting decks, porches and railings with weatherproof coatings for longevity.

Fence painting & staining

Protective finishes for wooden fences to prevent rot, UV damage and moisture penetration.

Power washing prep

Surface cleaning and pressure washing to remove dirt, mildew and peeling paint before coating.

Our Exterior Painting Process

Exterior work begins with a detailed inspection and preparation. We remove loose paint, wash surfaces, repair minor wood damage, and caulk cracks to ensure the new coating adheres properly and protects your home for years.

We apply high-quality primer when needed, especially on bare or weathered wood. Then we use premium exterior paints formulated to resist UV rays, mold, mildew, and harsh New England weather.

For decks and fences, we use professional-grade stains and sealers that penetrate and protect wood from moisture and sun damage.

Once complete, we perform a detailed final walkthrough to ensure even coverage, clean lines and a professional look that boosts your home's curb appeal.
