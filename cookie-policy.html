Last updated: January 2025

1. Introduction

This Cookie Policy explains how Lupa Services (“Company”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) uses cookies and similar tracking technologies when you visit our website. By continuing to browse or use our site, you agree to the use of cookies as described in this policy.

2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the website remember your actions and preferences over time, improving your user experience.

3. Types of Cookies We Use

3.1 Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the website to function properly. They cannot be disabled through our cookie settings.

3.2 Performance & Analytics Cookies

These cookies help us understand how visitors use the website and allow us to measure performance. We use them to improve usability and website experience.

3.3 Functional Cookies

Functional cookies enable enhanced features, such as remembering your preferences, location, or language selection.

3.4 Advertising & Marketing Cookies

These cookies may track browsing behavior to deliver personalized advertisements. If used, they will only be activated with your consent.

4. Cookies We May Use

Our website may use the following cookies or similar technologies:

  • Google Analytics
  • Facebook/Meta Pixel
  • Session tracking cookies
  • Functional preference cookies

We may update this list as our website evolves.

5. Third-Party Cookies

Third-party services integrated into our website may also set cookies, such as:

  • YouTube video embeds
  • Google Maps
  • Social media platforms
  • Analytics and tracking tools

These third parties have their own privacy and cookie policies.

6. How You Can Manage or Disable Cookies

You can manage, block, or delete cookies at any time through your browser settings. Below are links to cookie settings for common browsers:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

If you disable cookies, certain website features may not function correctly.

7. Your Rights

7.1 GDPR (EU Users)

Users in the European Union have the right to:

  • Withdraw cookie consent at any time
  • Request deletion or restriction of data processing
  • Access personal data collected through cookies

7.2 CCPA (California Users)

California residents have the right to:

  • Know which cookies collect personal information
  • Opt out of data sale or sharing
  • Request deletion of personal data collected via cookies

7.3 LGPD (Brazil)

Brazilian users have the right to:

  • Access, correct, and delete personal data
  • Withdraw consent for cookie usage
  • Request information on third-party cookie sharing
