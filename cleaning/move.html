Move-In & Move-Out Cleaning

Lupa Cleaning provides detailed move-in and move-out cleaning services to help you get your security deposit back, prepare for listing photos, or move into a fresh, sanitized home. We focus on the areas that landlords, property managers and new owners notice most.

What we clean during move-in/move-out

A top-to-bottom reset of empty or nearly empty homes and apartments.

Inside cabinets & drawers

Wiping and sanitizing inside kitchen and bathroom cabinets and drawers once they’re emptied.

Appliance interior & exterior

Deep cleaning of oven, stovetop, fridge exterior, microwave interior and other major appliances.

Bathrooms reset

Scrubbing tubs, showers, grout, toilets, sinks and fixtures so everything is ready for the next resident.

Baseboards, doors & trim

Dusting and wiping doors, frames, switches, outlets and baseboards throughout the property.

Windowsills & tracks

Cleaning window ledges, sills and accessible tracks to remove dust, dirt and buildup.

Floors throughout

Vacuuming and mopping all floors, including closets and corners that are usually hidden by furniture.

Our Move-In & Move-Out Cleaning Process

Moving is stressful enough. We start by confirming which rooms and areas are included, your move date and whether the home will be fully empty or partially furnished.

For move-out cleanings, we focus on what landlords and property managers look for: inside cabinets, appliances, bathrooms, walls, doors, baseboards and floors. We work methodically from top to bottom so dust and debris are removed, not moved around.

For move-in cleanings, we thoroughly sanitize kitchens and bathrooms, wipe inside storage areas, clean high-touch surfaces and refresh floors so your new space feels truly clean before you unpack.

We bring our own supplies and check list, and can coordinate with your schedule, keys or lockbox to make move day as simple and smooth as possible.
