Trim & Door Painting

Lupa Painting refreshes interior trim, baseboards, doors and casings with clean, sharp finishes that make your walls and floors look better instantly. Crisp trim lines and smooth enamel coatings help your entire home feel newer and more polished.

What we can paint and refinish

Baseboards, doors, casings, crown molding and more — with durable enamel finishes built to last.

Baseboards & shoe molding

Cleaning, caulking and repainting baseboards for a neat transition between your walls and floors.

Interior doors & frames

Repainting flat and paneled doors, jambs and frames with smooth, durable trim enamels.

Window trim & casings

Detail work around windows, including sills and casings, to brighten and define your openings.

Crown molding & chair rails

Highlighting decorative trim with crisp lines and finishes that match or complement your walls.

Stair railings & balusters

Sanding, priming and coating handrails, spindles and newel posts for a cleaner look on your stairs.

Touch-ups & repairs

Filling nail holes, caulking gaps and touching up chipped trim to restore a continuous, finished look.

Our Trim & Door Painting Process

Trim takes a lot of abuse from day-to-day life, so we begin with careful cleaning and preparation. We remove dust, light dirt and oils, then sand glossy areas to help the new coatings adhere.

We handle caulking and repairs by filling nail holes, patching small dents, and caulking gaps between trim and walls. This step is key for getting that “finished carpentry” look, even on older trim.

Next, we apply high-adhesion primer where needed, especially on bare wood or previously stained and varnished surfaces. Then we use professional trim enamels in semi-gloss or satin, depending on your preference, for a smooth, wipeable finish.

We finish with a detailed cut-in and inspection, checking lines where trim meets walls and floors, and making touch-ups so everything looks clean, even and consistent throughout your home.
