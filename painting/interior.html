Interior Painting Services

Lupa Painting provides professional interior painting services for homes, apartments and condos across Marlborough, Worcester and Greater Boston. From full-room repaints to accent walls and trim refinishing, our trained team focuses on clean lines, smooth finishes and minimal disruption to your routine.

What we can paint inside your home

Walls, ceilings, trim, doors, accent walls and more — with clean prep and premium materials.

Full-room repaint

Complete repaint of walls and ceilings with smooth, uniform finish and sharp cut lines.

Accent walls & feature colors

Highlight a living room, bedroom or entryway with bold accent colors and modern finishes.

Trim, doors & baseboards

Sanding, caulking and repainting of trim, frames, baseboards and interior doors for a crisp look.

Ceilings & repairs

Minor drywall repairs, stain blocking and ceiling painting to brighten up dark or damaged areas.

Kitchens & bathrooms

Durable, moisture-resistant coatings in high-traffic and high-humidity areas of your home.

Apartments & condos

Turnover and refresh painting between tenants or before listing your property for sale.

Our Interior Painting Process

A great paint job starts long before the first coat. Lupa Painting follows a detailed prep and protection process to keep your home clean and your finishes flawless. We move and cover furniture, protect floors and mask fixtures to avoid splatters and dust.

Next, we handle surface preparation: sanding glossy areas, repairing small cracks and nail holes, caulking gaps and spot-priming where needed. This step is essential for a smooth finish and long-lasting results.

For the actual painting, we work with quality, low-VOC paints from trusted brands, helping you choose the right sheen and color for each room: matte or eggshell for walls, semi-gloss for trim and doors, and flat finishes for ceilings. We apply multiple coats as needed, checking coverage and uniformity in natural and artificial light.

At the end, we perform a detailed final walkthrough, clean the work area, remove masking and tape, and make small touch-ups so you can enjoy your freshly painted space immediately.
