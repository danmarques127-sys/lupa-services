Our Story

Built on Craftsmanship.
Trusted for Consistent Painting Results.

Lupa Painting was built to deliver what homeowners and businesses want most: clean, reliable, high-quality painting results. We focus on the details that matter most—surface preparation, protection, sharp cut lines, proper curing, and durable finishes for every interior and exterior painting project.

The company is led by Lucas Reis, a Brazilian professional who moved to the United States about 7 years ago. Since then, he has specialized in residential and commercial painting, including interiors, exteriors, cabinets, trim, and surface restoration, bringing a disciplined process and a high standard of workmanship to every job.

Our Process

SIMPLE. STRUCTURED. RELIABLE.

1

Walkthrough & Estimate

We review the project scope, surfaces, prep needs, timeline, and expectations before providing a clear estimate.

2

Protection & Prep

We protect floors and furniture, mask edges, and complete patching, sanding, caulking, and priming where needed.

3

Paint & Finish

Our team applies paint professionally with clean cut lines, even coverage, and consistent sheen throughout the space.

4

Final Walkthrough

We review the finished work with you, complete touch-ups if needed, and leave the space clean and ready to enjoy.
