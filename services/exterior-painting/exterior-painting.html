Choosing the Right Exterior Painting Approach

Some homes need a standard repaint, while others need heavier prep, repairs, or primer work first. We help you choose the right approach based on surface condition, exposure, and the finish life you want.

Exterior Paint Care & Maintenance Tips

  • Wash dirt and mildew buildup off exterior surfaces seasonally
  • Check caulk lines around trim, windows, and doors once a year
  • Touch up chips or exposed areas before moisture gets in
  • Let us know if you see peeling, cracking, or fading spreading
Pro Tip: Catching small exterior paint failures early helps prevent bigger surface damage and keeps repaint costs lower over time.

Repaint

Best for exteriors with stable paint but visible fading, chalking, or color aging that need a refreshed, durable finish.

Prep-Heavy Restoration

Ideal when peeling, cracking, or weather wear is widespread and surfaces need scraping, sanding, caulking, and primer before painting.

Color Update

Great for curb appeal upgrades using new siding, trim, and shutter color combinations that modernize the look of your home.
