Our Story

Built on Real Jobs, Real Homes, and Real Commitment

Lupa Services Painting was founded in 2018, starting out with subcontracted exterior painting and carpentry work. Those first years were spent on real job sites, learning how to prepare surfaces the right way, solve problems on the spot, and deliver a finish that truly lasts.

Over time, that day-to-day experience grew into a solid foundation of knowledge. We refined our processes, expanded our technical skills, and focused on offering superior results on every project. One thing never changed: our clear commitment to serious, high–quality work, always with dedication and respect for each client.

As the projects grew, so did the trust. What began as subcontracting evolved into a painting company recognized for efficiency, organization, and excellence on every job. Today, Lupa Services Painting keeps growing with the same dedication we had at the beginning — but with stronger results and a service standard that truly stands out in the market.

Meet the Founder

My Story & Why I’m in This Field

My name is Lucas, and my journey in construction started long before Lupa Services officially existed. I’ve always been passionate about creating, transforming, and delivering work that truly makes a difference in people’s lives.

I first entered this field working with painting and carpentry, developing technical skills, precision, and a strong sense of responsibility on every job. For me, being in this trade is more than a professional choice — it’s a purpose.

I believe in the impact that a well–done job has on a client’s life: bringing safety, beauty, and long–term value to their space. That combination of technique, care, and dedication is what motivates me every day and gives real meaning to what I do.

“Every project is personal to me — it’s not just about paint on the walls, it’s about respect for the home and the people who live in it.”
