About the Company

PROFESSIONAL PAINTING DONE RIGHT.

Built on integrity, craftsmanship, and attention to detail, LUPA PAINTING helps homeowners and businesses across MetroWest Massachusetts transform interiors and exteriors with quality finishes that last. We do more than apply paint — we prepare surfaces correctly, protect your property, and deliver clean lines with a professional process from start to finish.

Our team is made up of skilled painters who treat every home like it is their own and every client like a partner. From residential interior and exterior painting to offices, cabinets, decks, and commercial spaces, we deliver reliable results on time, on budget, and without shortcuts.
