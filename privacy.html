Section 01

Information We Collect

Lupa Painting may collect information that you voluntarily provide when you use this website, request an estimate, contact the business, call, text, or submit a form.

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Property address or project location
  • Project details, scope, photos, or messages you choose to provide

We may also collect limited technical information automatically through normal website operation, such as browser type, device type, referring pages, and general usage activity.

Section 02

How We Use Information

Information collected through the website may be used to operate the site, respond to inquiries, provide estimates, communicate about services, schedule appointments, and improve the user experience.

  • Responding to estimate requests and contact submissions
  • Communicating by phone, text, or email about requested services
  • Reviewing project scope and preparing quotes
  • Improving website performance, navigation, and content
  • Maintaining business records related to inquiries and services

Lupa Painting does not collect personal information through the website unless you choose to provide it.

Lupa Painting does not sell personal information submitted through this website.

Information may be shared only when reasonably necessary to operate the business, provide requested services, comply with legal obligations, or support normal website and communication functions.

  • When required by law, legal process, or to protect rights and safety

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to inquiries, maintain business records, follow up on requested services, comply with legal obligations, and support normal business operations.
