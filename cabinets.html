Cabinet Painting & Refinishing

Lupa Painting transforms tired kitchen and bathroom cabinets with durable, smooth finishes that look and feel like new. Our cabinet painting process is designed to upgrade the look of your home without the cost and disruption of a full replacement.

Cabinet painting & refinishing options

From kitchens and bathrooms to built-ins and islands, we give your cabinets a factory-smooth, long-lasting finish.

Kitchen cabinet repaint

Complete color change or refresh for upper and lower kitchen cabinets, doors and drawer fronts.

Bathroom vanities

Moisture-resistant coatings for vanities and storage cabinets in bathrooms and powder rooms.

Islands & accent pieces

Two-tone kitchens and bold island colors to create a custom designer look without replacing cabinets.

Built-ins & laundry rooms

Painting of built-in shelving, mudroom cubbies and laundry room cabinetry for a cohesive finish.

Cabinet door refinishing

Careful cleaning, sanding, priming and painting of cabinet doors and drawer fronts off-site or on-site.

Hardware & minor repairs

Filling old hardware holes, adjusting doors and installing new pulls or knobs for a complete refresh.

Our Cabinet Painting Process

Great cabinet finishes start with careful prep and protection. We label and remove doors and drawers, protect countertops, appliances and floors, and create a tidy work area to control dust and overspray.

Next, we perform thorough cleaning and sanding to remove grease and shine, repair minor dings and imperfections, and caulk gaps where needed. We then apply high-adhesion primers designed specifically for cabinet and trim surfaces.

For the finish coats, we use professional-grade, low-VOC enamel paints that cure to a hard, smooth surface. Depending on your project, we brush and roll or spray for a factory-like look with minimal texture.

Once the coatings have cured, we reinstall doors and drawers, adjust hinges, and install any new hardware you’ve selected. We complete a detailed final walkthrough with you, making touch-ups and ensuring your new cabinet finish looks clean, even and consistent.
