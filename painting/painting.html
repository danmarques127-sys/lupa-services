Before we start, we carefully cover floors, furniture, and belongings with plastic and drop cloths. For exteriors, we protect windows, plants, and hardscape as needed. Our goal is to leave your space clean, with paint only where it is supposed to be.

Proper prep is one of the most important parts of our work. We typically clean surfaces, scrape loose paint, sand where needed, repair small cracks and holes, caulk gaps, and prime as necessary. This helps the new paint bond correctly and last longer.
